# Parcel Logistics – 12 Customer Discovery Questions

1. When did you last contact the delivery company about a delayed parcel?
2. What information were you trying to find out?
3. How did you contact customer support?
4. How long did it take to receive an answer?
5. How many parcels have been delayed for you in the last three months?
6. Tell me about the last parcel that arrived damaged.
7. What did you do after discovering the damage?
8. How was your complaint handled?
9. Have you had difficulty tracking a parcel? What happened?
10. What do you normally do when tracking stops updating?
11. Have you contacted support more than once about the same parcel? Why?
12. What was the most frustrating delivery experience you had recently?

These questions focus on actual past behaviour and experiences rather than asking whether customers like an AI assistant.
