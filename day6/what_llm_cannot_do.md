# What an LLM Cannot Do for a Parcel Customer

An AI language model can help customers understand delivery policies, explain tracking events, collect information for a support request and guide customers through standard procedures. It is not a substitute for verified operational systems, accountable employees or authoritative decisions.

An LLM cannot independently confirm the physical location of a parcel. It can only report tracking information supplied by an authorized tracking system, and that information may be delayed or incomplete.

It cannot guarantee a delivery date. Weather, traffic, customs inspections, depot capacity and unexpected operational events can change delivery estimates.

It cannot inspect a damaged parcel through text alone or determine who caused the damage. A customer must provide evidence, and an authorized claims process must assess the claim.

It cannot approve refunds, compensation, address changes or claims unless a trusted business system explicitly authorizes the action. It should explain the policy and route the request to an authorized workflow.

It cannot safely invent missing tracking details, customer identities, policy exceptions or delivery records. When information is missing, it should say so and ask for the tracking ID or transfer the case to a human.

It cannot guarantee that every answer is current unless it retrieves the latest approved policy and operational data. Old documents or incorrect source records can produce misleading answers.

It cannot replace human judgment in unusual, sensitive or disputed cases. Complaints involving financial loss, legal issues, safety concerns, repeated delivery failures or vulnerable customers should be escalated.

## Example: small local model failure and hosted model success

A small local model may fail to interpret a long, multi-part customer complaint that includes a tracking history, a damage report and several policy conditions. It may overlook a condition or confuse the order of events.

A larger hosted model may succeed because it has greater model capacity and can be configured with a larger context window. This is not guaranteed: retrieval quality, prompt design, source accuracy and evaluation still matter.

## Customer safeguards

- Retrieve answers from approved policy sources.
- Show the policy evidence behind important answers.
- Never claim a parcel was delivered without verified tracking evidence.
- Do not expose another customer's personal information.
- Ask for only the minimum information needed.
- Escalate uncertain, disputed or high-impact cases.
- Log actions and make it clear when a human must decide.

**Customer promise:** The assistant can help customers find information faster, but it must be honest about uncertainty and must not pretend to have powers or information it does not possess.
